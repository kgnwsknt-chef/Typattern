Tardigrades, often called water bears, are tiny animals that are
usually less than a millimeter long. They live almost everywhere,
from mountain tops to the deep sea, and many of them can be found in
the damp moss of an ordinary garden. When their surroundings dry out,
they pull in their legs, lose most of the water in their bodies, and
enter a state in which their life processes nearly stop. In this form
they can survive extreme cold, strong radiation, and even a short
trip to outer space. When water returns, they slowly wake up and
carry on as if nothing had happened.
